Workstation notes

Keep the task in front. Let the room do the reminding.

Most posture tools compete with the work. They buzz, badge, and wait to be dismissed. The habit stays because the signal lives somewhere else, on a wrist, in a notification shade, off to the side of attention.

The better loop is quieter. Sit as you would. Write. When you drift toward the screen, or lean left or right, the display itself darkens. Stay there long enough and the environment insists. Sit back, and the page returns.

No dashboard. No score. Calibration in the first seconds, then the workstation becomes the interface.
