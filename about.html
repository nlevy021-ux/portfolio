About

Noah Levy

I design software interfaces for dense, high-stakes workflows—turning ambiguous goals and model output into clear actions operators can trust.

My background spans interaction design, applied machine learning, and a studio practice in photography and perception. That mix is the point: I treat AI as a product surface, not a novelty, and I use visual systems thinking to simplify complexity without sanding off power.

Recent work includes cognitive telemetry for racing, routing and comparison tools for multi-path plans, gaze-based reading feedback, post-op wound triage, and live speech coaching—each built end-to-end from problem framing through interaction design and working UI.

Focus

  • Interaction & visual design for data-dense products
  • AI → usable interface (triage, feedback, decision support)
  • Prototyping & front-end implementation (HTML/CSS/JS, React)
  • Python / ML product loops (vision, audio, sensing)
